# Enterprise Student Record and Academic Management Platform

**Unit 1 Mini-Project: Design Thinking and Product Planning**

## Overview
This project proposes a centralized platform for managing student records and academic information in a higher education institution. It is a planning and product-discovery project, designed to prepare the product for Agile development in later units.

## Problem
Student admissions, course registration, attendance, examinations, and results are often recorded in separate spreadsheets or paper registers. This can lead to duplicate records, slow retrieval, inconsistent data, delayed reports, and unnecessary administrative effort.

## Proposed solution
A role-based platform that maintains student profiles, course registrations, attendance, examination marks, results, and academic reports in one organized system.

## Primary users
- Students
- Faculty members
- Academic administrators
- Examination cell staff
- IT support personnel

## Unit 1 scope
This repository contains the product-planning documents, requirements, design-thinking analysis, personas, roadmap, architecture concept, and initial repository structure. It is not yet a production-ready application; implementation, security testing, and deployment are planned for later phases.
